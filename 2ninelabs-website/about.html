Who We Are

A Boutique Advisory Firm Built for Complex Missions

We are a boutique advisory firm specializing in AI-driven modernization, operational transformation, and institutional resilience. With more than two decades of experience across enterprise sales, emerging technologies, healthcare, education, and community organizations, we help clients navigate complexity with clarity, strategy, and practical execution.

Our work sits at the intersection of technology, human systems, and mission-driven impact — enabling organizations to modernize without losing their identity or purpose.

"We don't sell technology. We help organizations become stronger, more efficient, and better prepared for the future — through practical strategy and disciplined execution."

2-Nine Labs operates across two primary verticals: community and faith-based institutions pursuing preservation, modernization, and grant readiness; and rural healthcare organizations navigating federal transformation funding through the CMS Rural Health Transformation Program.

What Guides Us

Our Operating Principles

These aren't aspirational values on a wall. They're the commitments that shape how we work with every client.

Legacy First

We believe institutional identity is an asset, not a liability. Modernization that erases what makes an organization distinctive isn't transformation — it's replacement.

Practical Over Theoretical

We deliver roadmaps that organizations can actually execute. We don't hand off a strategy deck and disappear. Implementation support is part of how we work.

Senior Engagement, Always

Every engagement is direct. No junior team, no offshore handoff, no account manager as the intermediary. You work with the person who built the strategy.

Funding-Aware Strategy

We understand how federal and foundation funding works. We build grant readiness and eligibility documentation into our engagements — not as an afterthought, but as a core deliverable.

Community-Centered Impact

Our primary clients serve communities. We take that responsibility seriously. The organizations we help don't just improve internally — they become stronger anchors for the people they serve.

Transparency Without Jargon

We communicate clearly about what we're doing, why we're doing it, and what success looks like. No buzzword strategies. No inflated scope. Just direct, honest engagement.

How We Engage

The 2-Nine Labs Approach

Every engagement follows the same discipline — regardless of client size, sector, or complexity.

01
Discovery

A focused strategy session to understand your organization, its history, current operational challenges, and the outcomes that matter most to leadership.

02
Diagnosis

A structured readiness assessment covering operations, technology, funding eligibility, and institutional knowledge. Gap analysis delivered in plain language.

03
Delivery

A prioritized modernization roadmap with specific recommendations, funding pathway mapping, and implementation support — with accountability built in from day one.

04

For clients who want continued engagement, we provide advisory retainers, grant tracking, and implementation checkpoints — staying involved as your organization evolves.
